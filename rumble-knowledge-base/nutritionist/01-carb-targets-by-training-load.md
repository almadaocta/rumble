# Carbohydrate Targets by Training Load

## Overview

Daily carbohydrate needs for cyclists vary dramatically based on training load. A rest day and a 5-hour ride day have completely different fueling requirements. "Fuel for the work required" — match carbohydrate intake to the demands of each day.

## Daily Carbohydrate Guidelines by Training Day Type

These targets are based on body weight in kg, following guidelines from Burke et al. (2011) and Jeukendrup (2017).

### Rest Day / Very Light Activity
- **Carbs:** 3-5 g/kg/day
- **Example (75kg rider):** 225-375g carbs
- **Context:** No training or only a short easy walk/spin. Focus on recovery nutrition from previous day. Lower overall caloric intake.

### Light Training Day (Zone 1-2, <1.5 hours)
- **Carbs:** 5-7 g/kg/day
- **Example (75kg rider):** 375-525g carbs
- **Context:** Easy endurance ride or recovery spin. Moderate fueling. No need for during-ride carbs if <90 min.

### Moderate Training Day (Mixed zones, 1.5-3 hours, TSS 60-120)
- **Carbs:** 6-8 g/kg/day
- **Example (75kg rider):** 450-600g carbs
- **Context:** Sweet spot session, tempo ride, or moderate endurance. Include pre-ride and post-ride carb-focused meals.

### Hard Training Day (High intensity, 2-4 hours, TSS 120-250)
- **Carbs:** 8-10 g/kg/day
- **Example (75kg rider):** 600-750g carbs
- **Context:** Threshold intervals, VO2max sessions, or long hard rides. During-ride fueling becomes important (30-60g/h). Post-ride recovery meal critical within 30-60 minutes.

### Very Hard / Race Day (TSS >250 or race)
- **Carbs:** 10-12 g/kg/day
- **Example (75kg rider):** 750-900g carbs
- **Context:** Long race, gran fondo, or extreme training day. Carb loading in 24-48h prior. Aggressive during-ride fueling (60-90g/h). Prioritize fast-absorbing carbs.

## Practical Application

### How to Hit the Targets

A 75kg rider on a hard training day (8g/kg = 600g carbs):
- Breakfast: Oatmeal with banana and honey = ~100g carbs
- Pre-ride snack: Toast with jam = ~50g carbs
- During ride (2.5h): Energy drink + gels = ~120g carbs
- Post-ride recovery: Rice with chicken + fruit juice = ~100g carbs
- Lunch: Pasta with sauce = ~100g carbs
- Snack: Rice cakes = ~40g carbs
- Dinner: Rice, bread, vegetables = ~90g carbs
- Total: ~600g

### Carbohydrate Periodization

Not every day should be high-carb. Match carbs to the training:
- **Train high (fuel the work):** Before and during key sessions (threshold, VO2max, race) — ensure full glycogen stores and during-ride carbs.
- **Train low (when appropriate):** Some easy Zone 2 sessions can be done with lower carb availability to promote fat oxidation adaptations. Not recommended for every session — only easy aerobic rides.
- **Never train low for key sessions:** Threshold, VO2max, and race-pace sessions require full carb availability for proper execution and adaptation.

### Weight Management Considerations

If the athlete is targeting weight loss:
- Create a small caloric deficit (300-500 kcal/day) primarily on rest days and easy days
- NEVER restrict carbs on hard training days — this impairs performance and recovery
- Frame as power-to-weight, not just weight: "If your FTP drops more than weight drops, you're losing the wrong thing"
- Rate of loss: no more than 0.5 kg/week during training periods
- Monitor for signs of LEA (low energy availability): persistent fatigue, mood changes, declining performance
