# Pre-Ride and Post-Ride Nutrition

## Overview

What you eat before and after a ride directly affects performance, recovery, and adaptation. Pre-ride nutrition ensures adequate fuel availability. Post-ride nutrition accelerates glycogen resynthesis and muscle repair.

## Pre-Ride Nutrition

### 3-4 Hours Before
- **Goal:** Top off glycogen stores. Full meal.
- **Carbs:** 1-4 g/kg body weight (75-300g for a 75kg rider)
- **Protein:** 15-25g
- **Fat:** Moderate (not excessive — slows digestion)
- **Fiber:** Low-moderate (avoid high-fiber foods close to ride)
- **Examples:**
  - Oatmeal with banana, honey, and eggs
  - Toast with peanut butter and jam + yogurt
  - Rice with chicken and vegetables
  - Pasta with light sauce

### 1-2 Hours Before
- **Goal:** Easily digestible carbs. Small snack.
- **Carbs:** 30-50g
- **Protein:** Minimal (5-10g)
- **Fat/Fiber:** Low
- **Examples:**
  - Banana + energy bar
  - Toast with honey
  - Rice cake with jam
  - Small smoothie (banana + juice)

### 30 Minutes Before
- **Goal:** Quick energy if needed. Optional.
- **Carbs:** 20-30g simple carbs
- **Examples:** Gel, sports drink, banana, dates

### Fasted Riding
- **When appropriate:** Easy Zone 2 rides under 90 minutes, as part of a "train low" strategy to promote fat oxidation adaptations
- **When NOT appropriate:** Any session with intensity (threshold, VO2max, race-pace). Key sessions require fuel.
- **Caution:** Fasted riding is a tool, not a default. Chronic underfueling risks RED-S and impaired adaptation.

## Post-Ride Nutrition

### The Recovery Window

Glycogen resynthesis rate is highest in the first 30-60 minutes post-exercise. While the "anabolic window" isn't as narrow as once believed, eating sooner still matters — especially if training again within 24 hours.

### Within 30-60 Minutes Post-Ride
- **Carbs:** 1.0-1.2 g/kg body weight (75-90g for a 75kg rider)
- **Protein:** 20-30g (0.3-0.4 g/kg)
- **Ideal carb:protein ratio:** ~3:1 to 4:1
- **Examples:**
  - Recovery shake (whey protein + banana + oats + milk)
  - Chocolate milk (surprisingly effective — good carb:protein ratio)
  - Rice with chicken and fruit juice
  - Sandwich with turkey/chicken + fruit

### 2 Hours Post-Ride
- **Full meal** with balanced macros
- Prioritize carbs if next training session is within 24 hours
- Include protein (20-30g) and vegetables
- **Example:** Pasta with meat sauce + salad + bread

### Multiple Sessions Per Day
When training twice in one day, aggressive recovery nutrition is critical:
- Immediate post-session 1: 1.2 g/kg carbs + 25g protein
- Continue high carb intake (1.0 g/kg every 2 hours) until session 2
- Start fueling early in session 2

## Recovery Nutrition by Session Type

| Session Type | Recovery Urgency | Carb Priority | Protein Priority |
|-------------|-----------------|---------------|-----------------|
| Easy Zone 2 | Low | Moderate | Moderate |
| Sweet spot / tempo | Medium | High | High |
| Threshold intervals | High | High | High |
| VO2max intervals | High | Very high | High |
| Long ride (3+ hours) | High | Very high | High |
| Gym session | Medium | Moderate | Very high |
| Rest day (no ride) | Low | Low-moderate | Moderate |

## Hydration — Pre and Post

### Pre-Ride
- Drink 5-7 ml/kg body weight 2-4 hours before (375-525ml for 75kg)
- Urine should be pale yellow before starting

### Post-Ride
- Weigh before and after to estimate fluid loss
- Replace 150% of weight lost (e.g., lost 1kg → drink 1.5L over next 2-4 hours)
- Include sodium with rehydration fluids to improve fluid retention
