# Workout Library — Structured Cycling Sessions

## Overview

Structured workouts organized by target zone and training purpose. Each includes duration, intervals, zone targets, RPE, and the specific adaptation it develops. All power targets expressed as % of FTP.

## Zone 1 — Active Recovery Workouts

### AR-1: Recovery Spin
- **Duration:** 45-60 min
- **Structure:** Spin at <55% FTP. Cadence 85-95 rpm. No hard pedaling.
- **RPE:** 1-2
- **Purpose:** Recovery without additional training stress. Promotes blood flow.
- **When:** Day after a hard session or race.

## Zone 2 — Endurance Workouts

### END-1: Basic Endurance
- **Duration:** 2-3 hours
- **Structure:** Steady riding at 60-70% FTP. Cadence 85-95 rpm.
- **RPE:** 2-3
- **TSS estimate:** 80-130
- **Purpose:** Aerobic base development. Ride easy, ride long.

### END-2: Endurance with Bursts
- **Duration:** 2-2.5 hours
- **Structure:** Ride at 60-70% FTP. Include 10x 8-second bursts (out of saddle, ~80% max sprint effort, cadence >105 rpm) distributed throughout.
- **RPE:** 2-3 (with brief Zone 7 spikes)
- **TSS estimate:** 90-120
- **Purpose:** Endurance base + neuromuscular activation without significant fatigue.

### END-3: Long Endurance
- **Duration:** 4-5 hours
- **Structure:** Steady 60-75% FTP. Include 3x 20-min at sweet spot (88-94% FTP) in hours 3-4.
- **RPE:** 2-3 (sweet spot segments RPE 4)
- **TSS estimate:** 180-250
- **Purpose:** Build durability. Train fueling strategy. End-of-ride fatigue resistance.

## Zone 3 — Tempo Workouts

### TEMP-1: Tempo Intervals
- **Duration:** 1.5-2 hours
- **Structure:** Warmup 15 min → 2x 30 min at 76-90% FTP, 5 min recovery between → Cooldown 15 min.
- **RPE:** 3-4
- **TSS estimate:** 90-120
- **Purpose:** Muscular endurance. Sustained power at moderate effort.

### TEMP-2: Progressive Tempo
- **Duration:** 1.5 hours
- **Structure:** Warmup → 40 min continuous, building power every 10 min: 75% → 80% → 85% → 90% FTP → Cooldown.
- **RPE:** 3 building to 4
- **TSS estimate:** 80-100
- **Purpose:** Pacing discipline. Mental focus at increasing intensity.

## Sweet Spot Workouts

### SS-1: Sweet Spot 3x12
- **Duration:** 1.25 hours
- **Structure:** Warmup 15 min → 3x 12 min at 88-94% FTP, 5 min recovery → Cooldown 15 min.
- **RPE:** 4
- **TSS estimate:** 70-85
- **Purpose:** FTP development with manageable fatigue. Time-efficient.

### SS-2: Sweet Spot 2x20
- **Duration:** 1.5 hours
- **Structure:** Warmup 15 min → 2x 20 min at 88-94% FTP, 5 min recovery → Cooldown 15 min.
- **RPE:** 4
- **TSS estimate:** 80-95
- **Purpose:** Sustained sweet spot. Builds mental and physical tolerance at sub-threshold.

### SS-3: Sweet Spot Over-Under
- **Duration:** 1.25 hours
- **Structure:** Warmup 15 min → 3x 12 min alternating 2 min at 90% FTP / 1 min at 105% FTP → 5 min recovery → Cooldown.
- **RPE:** 4-5
- **TSS estimate:** 80-90
- **Purpose:** Teaches lactate clearance. Simulates race surges while at threshold.

### SS-4: Sweet Spot Power Ramp
- **Duration:** 1 hour
- **Structure:** Warmup 10 min → 10 min at 75% → 10 min at 80% → 10 min at 85% → 20 min at 88-94% → Cooldown 5 min.
- **RPE:** 3 building to 4
- **TSS estimate:** 55-65
- **Purpose:** Progressive loading. Good early-season session to reintroduce intensity.

## Zone 4 — Threshold Workouts

### LT-1: Threshold 2x20
- **Duration:** 1.25 hours
- **Structure:** Warmup 15 min → 2x 20 min at 95-105% FTP, 5 min recovery → Cooldown.
- **RPE:** 5
- **TSS estimate:** 80-95
- **Purpose:** The classic threshold session. Directly raises FTP.

### LT-2: Threshold 3x15
- **Duration:** 1.25 hours
- **Structure:** Warmup 15 min → 3x 15 min at 95-100% FTP, 5 min recovery → Cooldown.
- **RPE:** 5
- **TSS estimate:** 80-90
- **Purpose:** Threshold work with slightly shorter intervals. Good for athletes building toward 2x20.

### LT-3: Threshold Crisscross
- **Duration:** 1.5 hours
- **Structure:** Warmup 15 min → 2x 15 min at 90% FTP with 30-second surges to 120% FTP every 2 min, never dropping below 85% FTP between surges → 5 min recovery → Cooldown.
- **RPE:** 5-6
- **TSS estimate:** 85-100
- **Purpose:** Lactate clearance under load. Simulates race dynamics at threshold.

## Zone 5 — VO2max Workouts

### VO2-1: Classic 5x5
- **Duration:** 1.25 hours
- **Structure:** Warmup 15 min → 5x 5 min at 106-120% FTP, 5 min recovery → Cooldown.
- **RPE:** 6-7
- **TSS estimate:** 80-95
- **Purpose:** VO2max development. The gold standard VO2max session.

### VO2-2: Short-Short Intervals
- **Duration:** 1 hour
- **Structure:** Warmup 15 min → 3 sets of (8x 30 sec ON at 130% FTP / 30 sec OFF at 50% FTP), 5 min between sets → Cooldown.
- **RPE:** 7
- **TSS estimate:** 65-80
- **Purpose:** Accumulate time at VO2max without sustained suffering. Research shows 30/30s effectively elevate VO2 when done in sufficient volume.

### VO2-3: Descending Intervals
- **Duration:** 1.25 hours
- **Structure:** Warmup 15 min → 5 min at 108% / 4 min at 112% / 3 min at 116% / 2 min at 120% → each with 5 min recovery → Cooldown.
- **RPE:** 6-7
- **TSS estimate:** 75-90
- **Purpose:** VO2max with increasing intensity. Mentally engaging.

## Zone 6-7 — Anaerobic and Sprint Workouts

### AC-1: Anaerobic Capacity Intervals
- **Duration:** 1 hour
- **Structure:** Warmup 15 min → 8x 1 min at 135-150% FTP, 2 min recovery → Cooldown.
- **RPE:** 8-9
- **TSS estimate:** 60-75
- **Purpose:** Anaerobic capacity. Ability to sustain maximal efforts for 1-3 minutes.

### NP-1: Sprint Intervals
- **Duration:** 1 hour
- **Structure:** Warmup 15 min → 6x 15-second all-out sprints from rolling start, 5 min full recovery → Cooldown.
- **RPE:** 10 (during sprints)
- **TSS estimate:** 40-55
- **Purpose:** Peak neuromuscular power. Race-finish preparation.

## Workout Selection by Phase

| Phase | Primary Workouts | Frequency |
|-------|-----------------|-----------|
| Base | END-1, END-3, SS-4 | 4-5 rides/week, mostly Zone 2 |
| Build | SS-1, SS-2, LT-1, VO2-1 | 3-4 rides/week, 2-3 intensity sessions |
| Peak | LT-3, VO2-2, AC-1, NP-1 | 3-4 rides/week, race-specific intensity |
| Taper | END-1, SS-1 (shortened), NP-1 | 2-3 rides/week, reduced volume |
| Recovery | AR-1, END-1 (shortened) | 2-3 easy rides |
