# Weekly Plan Templates by Phase and Hours

## Overview

Template weeks for different training phases and available hours. These are starting points — adjust based on the athlete's current CTL, fatigue, life stress, and response to training.

## Principles for Building a Week

1. **Never schedule hard rides on consecutive days** without a recovery-awareness reason.
2. **Key sessions first:** Place the 2-3 most important sessions, then fill around them.
3. **Coordinate with gym:** Never schedule heavy legs before key cycling intervals.
4. **Include rest days explicitly:** Rest is training.
5. **Weekly TSS should align with CTL trajectory:** Ramp rate no more than 5-7 CTL points/week.

## Taper Week (All Hour Budgets)

Reduce volume by 40-60% while maintaining intensity. Remove long rides. Keep 1-2 short intensity sessions.

| Day | Session | Duration | Notes |
|-----|---------|----------|-------|
| Mon | Rest | - | |
| Tue | SS-1 (shortened to 2x10) | 0:50 | Keep sharpness |
| Wed | Rest or very easy spin | 0:30 | |
| Thu | NP-1: 4x sprints | 0:45 | Neuromuscular activation |
| Fri | Rest | - | Complete rest |
| Sat | Easy opener ride + 2x 5min at FTP | 0:45 | Legs-open session |
| Sun | **RACE DAY** | - | Target TSB: +10 to +25 |

## Deload Week (Every 3-4 Weeks)

Reduce TSS by 40-50% from the previous 3-week average. Zone 1-2 only. No intensity.
