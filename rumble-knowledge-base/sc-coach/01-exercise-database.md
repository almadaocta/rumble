# Exercise Database — Strength Training for Cyclists

## Overview

Cycling-specific strength exercises targeting lower body power, single-leg stability, core for riding position, and injury prevention. Every exercise has a clear cycling benefit. Based on Rønnestad's evidence-based protocol for endurance cyclists.

## Primary Compound Exercises

### 1. Barbell Back Squat (or Smith Machine Half-Squat)
- **Sets/Reps:** 3 x 4-10 RM (vary across phases)
- **Execution:** Descend to at least 90° knee angle (half-squat used in Rønnestad studies). Maximal acceleration on concentric (up). Slow and controlled eccentric (down, 2-3 seconds).
- **Cycling benefit:** Primary driver of leg strength. Transfers to sustained pedaling power, climbing, and sprint initiation.
- **Form cues:** Feet shoulder-width, chest up, knees track over toes, drive through heels.
- **Progression:** Bodyweight → goblet squat → barbell back squat.
- **Safety:** Beginners should learn form with a qualified instructor before loading heavy.

### 2. Single-Leg Leg Press
- **Sets/Reps:** 3 x 6-10 RM per leg
- **Execution:** One foot on the platform. Full range of motion. Controlled throughout.
- **Cycling benefit:** Addresses left-right imbalances common in cyclists. Single-leg strength directly transfers to pedaling (which is inherently a single-leg activity).
- **Form cues:** Foot centered on platform, don't lock knee at top, control the negative.

### 3. Romanian Deadlift (RDL)
- **Sets/Reps:** 3 x 6-10
- **Execution:** Hinge at hips with slight knee bend. Bar tracks close to shins. Feel stretch in hamstrings. Squeeze glutes at top.
- **Cycling benefit:** Posterior chain strength (hamstrings, glutes). Prevents lower back pain common in cyclists. Improves pedaling power through the bottom of the stroke.
- **Form cues:** Neutral spine, push hips back, shoulders pulled back, bar close to body.

### 4. Single-Leg Hip Flexion
- **Sets/Reps:** 3 x 10-15 per leg
- **Execution:** Standing or hanging, drive knee up against resistance (cable, band, or ankle weight).
- **Cycling benefit:** Hip flexor strength for the upstroke. Often weak in cyclists who only push down.
- **Form cues:** Controlled movement, don't swing. Full range from hip extension to flexion.

### 5. Calf Raises (Toe Raises)
- **Sets/Reps:** 3 x 12-15
- **Execution:** Standing on edge of step. Full range: drop heels below platform, rise to full extension.
- **Cycling benefit:** Ankle stability and force transfer through the pedal. Prevents Achilles issues.
- **Form cues:** Slow and controlled. Pause at top. Don't bounce.

## Core Exercises

### 6. Plank (Front)
- **Duration:** 3 x 30-60 seconds
- **Cycling benefit:** Core stability for maintaining riding position. Prevents energy loss through torso movement during hard efforts.
- **Form cues:** Straight line from head to heels. Don't sag or pike. Breathe normally.

### 7. Side Plank
- **Duration:** 3 x 20-40 seconds per side
- **Cycling benefit:** Lateral core stability. Prevents hip drop and power leakage during seated climbing.

### 8. Dead Bug
- **Sets/Reps:** 3 x 8-10 per side
- **Execution:** Supine, arms extended, knees at 90°. Extend opposite arm and leg while maintaining flat lower back.
- **Cycling benefit:** Anti-extension core strength. Teaches core to resist movement — exactly what you need on the bike.

### 9. Pallof Press
- **Sets/Reps:** 3 x 10-12 per side
- **Execution:** Cable or band at chest height. Press arms straight forward, resisting rotation. Hold 2 seconds.
- **Cycling benefit:** Anti-rotation core strength. Stabilizes torso during out-of-saddle climbing and sprinting.

## Hip Stability and Injury Prevention

### 10. Clamshell
- **Sets/Reps:** 3 x 15-20 per side
- **Cycling benefit:** Gluteus medius activation. Prevents knee tracking inward (common cause of cycling knee pain).

### 11. Single-Leg Glute Bridge
- **Sets/Reps:** 3 x 10-12 per side
- **Cycling benefit:** Glute activation and hip extension strength. Addresses "dead glutes" from prolonged sitting.

### 12. Banded Lateral Walk
- **Sets/Reps:** 3 x 12-15 steps per direction
- **Cycling benefit:** Hip abductor activation. Stabilizes pelvis during pedaling.

## Upper Body (Maintenance Only)

### 13. Push-Up
- **Sets/Reps:** 3 x 10-15
- **Cycling benefit:** Maintains upper body for bike handling, crash protection, and riding position support.

### 14. Bent-Over Row
- **Sets/Reps:** 3 x 10-12
- **Cycling benefit:** Upper back strength to maintain aero position. Prevents rounded shoulders from cycling posture.

## Mobility Exercises

### 15. Hip Flexor Stretch (Half-Kneeling)
- **Duration:** 2 x 45-60 seconds per side
- **Cycling benefit:** Counteracts hip flexor tightness from cycling position.

### 16. Thoracic Spine Rotation
- **Sets/Reps:** 2 x 10 per side
- **Cycling benefit:** Counters thoracic kyphosis from cycling position.

### 17. Hamstring Stretch (Active)
- **Duration:** 2 x 30-45 seconds per side
- **Cycling benefit:** Maintains hamstring flexibility for pedaling range of motion.

### 18. Pigeon Stretch
- **Duration:** 2 x 45-60 seconds per side
- **Cycling benefit:** External hip rotation and glute flexibility. Relieves hip tightness.
