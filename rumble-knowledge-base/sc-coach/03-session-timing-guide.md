# Gym Session Timing Guide — Coordinating Strength with Cycling

## Overview

When you schedule gym sessions relative to cycling sessions matters significantly. Poor timing creates unnecessary fatigue that undermines both the gym work and the riding. Good timing allows both to coexist and enhance performance.

## The Core Rule

**Never schedule heavy legs before a key cycling session.** Squat day before VO2max intervals guarantees poor performance in both.

## Timing Guidelines

### Same Day: Gym + Ride

**Gym in the morning, ride in the afternoon/evening:**
- Minimum 6 hours between sessions
- Works well when the ride is Zone 2 endurance
- Not ideal if the ride includes threshold or VO2max intervals
- Eat and hydrate between sessions

**Ride in the morning, gym in the afternoon:**
- Works if the ride was easy (Zone 1-2)
- Not recommended if the ride was intense — legs will be pre-fatigued for the gym
- Post-ride nutrition becomes critical for gym performance

### Separate Days: Best Approach

**Ideal weekly pattern:**
- Hard ride day → Easy ride or rest → Gym → Easy ride → Hard ride

**Example for 2 gym sessions/week:**
| Day | AM Session | PM Session |
|-----|-----------|------------|
| Mon | Rest | Gym Session B |
| Tue | Key ride (VO2max or threshold) | - |
| Wed | Easy endurance | - |
| Thu | Gym Session B | Easy endurance |
| Fri | Rest or easy spin | - |
| Sat | Long ride | - |
| Sun | Easy endurance | - |

### Season-Specific Scheduling

| Training Phase | Gym Frequency | Placement |
|---------------|---------------|-----------|
| Off-season / base | 3x/week | Prioritize gym. Rides support, not lead. |
| Build | 2x/week | Gym on easy cycling days. Key rides have priority. |
| Peak / race | 1x per 7-10 days | Maintenance only. Never within 48h before a race. |
| Taper | 0-1x | Last gym session 5-7 days before target event. |
| Recovery week | 0-1x (light) | Bodyweight or light mobility only. |

## Recovery After Gym Sessions

### Expected Fatigue Timeline
- **0-6 hours:** Acute muscle fatigue. Legs feel heavy.
- **24-36 hours:** DOMS peaks (delayed onset muscle soreness). Normal.
- **48-72 hours:** Recovery for most athletes. Legs feel normal.

### Implications for Cycling
- Don't expect quality cycling output for 24-48 hours after a heavy gym session
- Plan easy rides or rest for the day after gym
- Nutrition is critical: 20-30g protein within 60 minutes post-gym

### Adapting Over Time
DOMS decreases significantly after 3-4 weeks of consistent strength training. Early weeks require more recovery between gym and hard rides. By week 6+, many athletes can ride quality sessions the day after gym.

## Common Mistakes

1. **Gym the day before key intervals:** Legs are compromised. The cycling session suffers.
2. **Heavy gym during taper:** Unnecessary fatigue before a race. Only light maintenance.
3. **Skipping gym during race season entirely:** Gains reverse within 4-8 weeks. Maintain with 1 session every 7-10 days.
4. **Too many exercises:** Cyclists need 4-6 exercises, not a bodybuilder's 12-exercise session. Get in, get strong, get out.
5. **Ignoring nutrition:** The gym session is wasted without adequate protein and carbs for recovery.
