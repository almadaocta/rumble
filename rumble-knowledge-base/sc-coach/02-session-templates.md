# Gym Session Templates for Cyclists

## Overview

Four session templates aligned with cycling training phases. Each session is 30-50 minutes. Based on Rønnestad's evidence-based protocol: compound lower body exercises, 3 sets, 4-10 RM range, maximal concentric acceleration, controlled eccentric.

## Session A — Anatomical Adaptation (Off-Season / Early Base)

**Goal:** Prepare muscles, tendons, and joints for heavier loading. Learn movement patterns.
**Duration:** 40-50 min | **Frequency:** 3x/week for 3-4 weeks

| # | Exercise | Sets x Reps | Rest | RPE | Notes |
|---|----------|-------------|------|-----|-------|
| 1 | Goblet Squat | 3 x 12-15 | 60s | 5-6 | Learn squat pattern with light load |
| 2 | Single-Leg Leg Press | 3 x 12-15 | 60s | 5-6 | Each leg separately |
| 3 | Romanian Deadlift | 3 x 12-15 | 60s | 5-6 | Light weight, focus on hip hinge |
| 4 | Calf Raises | 3 x 15 | 45s | 5 | Full range of motion |
| 5 | Plank | 3 x 30-45s | 45s | 4-5 | |
| 6 | Clamshell | 3 x 15/side | 30s | 4 | Band optional |
| 7 | Hip Flexor Stretch | 2 x 45s/side | - | - | Post-session |

## Session B — Max Strength (Late Base / Early Build)

**Goal:** Develop maximal strength. Heavy loads, lower reps.
**Duration:** 40-45 min | **Frequency:** 2x/week for 6-10 weeks

| # | Exercise | Sets x Reps | Rest | RPE | Notes |
|---|----------|-------------|------|-----|-------|
| 1 | Back Squat or Half-Squat | 3 x 4-6 RM | 3 min | 8-9 | Max acceleration concentric, 2-3s eccentric |
| 2 | Single-Leg Leg Press | 3 x 6-8 RM | 2 min | 8-9 | Each leg |
| 3 | Single-Leg Hip Flexion | 3 x 8-10/leg | 90s | 7-8 | Cable or band |
| 4 | Calf Raises | 3 x 10-12 | 60s | 7 | Add weight |
| 5 | Dead Bug | 3 x 8/side | 45s | 5-6 | |
| 6 | Pallof Press | 3 x 10/side | 45s | 6 | |

**Key principle:** Always attempt maximal acceleration during the concentric (lifting) phase, even when the weight moves slowly. This recruits fast-twitch fibers.

## Session C — Power (Build / Pre-Competition)

**Goal:** Convert max strength to cycling-specific power. Moderate loads, explosive execution.
**Duration:** 35-40 min | **Frequency:** 2x/week for 4-6 weeks

| # | Exercise | Sets x Reps | Rest | RPE | Notes |
|---|----------|-------------|------|-----|-------|
| 1 | Jump Squat (light load) | 3 x 6-8 | 2 min | 7-8 | 30-40% 1RM, maximum height |
| 2 | Single-Leg Leg Press (explosive) | 3 x 8/leg | 90s | 7 | Fast concentric, controlled eccentric |
| 3 | Step-Up with Knee Drive | 3 x 8/leg | 90s | 7 | Explosive drive up |
| 4 | Single-Leg Glute Bridge | 3 x 10/side | 60s | 6 | |
| 5 | Side Plank | 3 x 25s/side | 30s | 5 | |
| 6 | Thoracic Spine Rotation | 2 x 10/side | - | - | Mobility |

## Session D — Maintenance (Race Season)

**Goal:** Preserve strength gains with minimal fatigue. One session every 7-10 days.
**Duration:** 25-30 min | **Frequency:** 1x per 7-10 days

| # | Exercise | Sets x Reps | Rest | RPE | Notes |
|---|----------|-------------|------|-----|-------|
| 1 | Back Squat | 2 x 5-6 RM | 3 min | 8 | Same intensity as max strength, reduced volume |
| 2 | Single-Leg Leg Press | 2 x 6-8/leg | 2 min | 8 | |
| 3 | Plank | 2 x 30-45s | 30s | 5 | |
| 4 | Pigeon Stretch | 2 x 45s/side | - | - | Mobility |

**Critical:** Stopping strength training during race season reverses gains within 4-8 weeks. Maintenance prevents this. One session every 7-10 days is sufficient to maintain.

## Warmup Protocol (All Sessions)

1. 5 min light cardio (bike, rower, or walking)
2. 10x bodyweight squats
3. 10x walking lunges
4. 10x leg swings (front-back + side-to-side)
5. 1-2 warmup sets of first exercise at 50% and 70% working weight
